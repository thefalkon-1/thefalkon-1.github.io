Original Color Scheme (julian theme)
=====================================

Background:        #fff (white)
Code background:   #f5f5f5 (light gray)
Main text:         #4d4d4d (dark gray)
Headings:          #333 (charcoal)
Secondary text:    #666 (medium gray)
Muted text:        #999 (light gray - dates, tagline)
Borders:           #eee (very light gray)
Links/Accent:      #0e94ec (blue)
