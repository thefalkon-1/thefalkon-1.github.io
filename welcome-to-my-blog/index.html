Welcome to My Blog

Welcome! This is the first post on my new blog.

I've set up this site to share my thoughts on technology, programming, and various projects I'm working on. Expect posts about:

  • Interesting problems and their solutions

Why a Blog?

In an age of social media, I think there's still value in long-form writing. A blog gives me space to explore ideas in depth, without the constraints of character limits or algorithmic feeds.
